Radar provides SDKs and APIs for location verification, fraud detection, geofencing, and maps. Choose a product to see how it works and run its quickstart, or jump to a task below.

  <Card title="Geo-compliance" icon="shield-check" href="/geo-compliance/overview">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Fraud" icon="user-shield" href="/fraud/overview">
    Assess device and network risk, and detect location spoofing, VPNs, and account abuse.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Geofencing" icon="location-dot" href="/geofencing/overview">
    Detect geofence entries and place visits, track trips, and range beacons.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Maps" icon="map" href="/maps/overview">
    Display maps, build store locators, and autocomplete, geocode, and validate addresses.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
